# Footer park artwork

The footer uses separate desktop and mobile WebP images:

- `public/assets/footer-products-park-v2-desktop.webp` (1536 × 1024)
- `public/assets/footer-products-park-v2-mobile.webp` (1024 × 1536)

Both were made with the built-in imagegen tool. The existing `footer-products-v5.webp` and `footer-products-mobile.webp` were the park and staging references. Their old product lineup was removed, real Enjoyful product cutouts from the game's catalog-derived atlases were placed on the picnic blanket, and the resulting collages were edited for natural lighting, contact shadows, fabric, and picnic details. The final PNGs were encoded as WebP with Sharp. Product labels in the finished scene are part of generated artwork, so the product pages remain the source for exact packaging details.

## Final image prompts

Mobile edit target: the portrait park collage. Style reference: `footer-products-mobile.webp`.

> Use case: compositing. Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET, the current portrait mobile park footer collage with five real Enjoyful product packages. Image 2 is the STYLE AND STAGING REFERENCE, the previous footer photo: study its believable outdoor picnic product still-life, flowers, wicker props, fruit, warm sunlight, shadows, and irregular depths. Rework Image 1 into a premium photorealistic advertising photograph, NOT a neat row of isolated cutouts. Preserve the same portrait park, light white fade at the very top, blue sky, trees, grass, picnic blanket and five real product identities, packaging shapes, colors and visible label artwork. Physically stage these five products in a loose natural arrangement on the fabric, at varied slight depths and small rotations, with accurate contact shadows, sunlight matching the scene, edge bounce light, subtle occlusion from fabric folds, and realistic perspective. Add tasteful small picnic details around them: daisies, a wicker basket, a few citrus slices, berries, leaves, and a folded linen napkin, like Image 2. Keep products central and individually readable, lower grass area clear enough for live white footer title and links; no printed footer text. Crucially do not invent or redraw product branding, do not create additional product bottles, no people, UI, watermark, or border. Keep the original park/white-top feel, high resolution and photographic realism. The change should be obvious: an integrated still-life with surrounding props, not bottles pasted onto a flat background.

Desktop edit target: the wide park collage. Style reference: `footer-products-v5.webp`.

> Use case: compositing. Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET, current wide desktop park footer collage with seven real Enjoyful product packages. Image 2 is STYLE AND STAGING REFERENCE, previous desktop footer photo: study its convincing outdoor picnic product still-life, natural highlights, shadows, wicker basket, flowers and fruit. Rework Image 1 into a premium photorealistic campaign still-life, not a perfectly uniform row of pasted cutouts. Keep the same 3:2 wide park and picnic environment, the light WHITE blend at the top, blue sky, trees, flowering grass and blanket. Keep the seven Enjoyful products from Image 1, their real package shapes, identities, artwork and colors; arrange loosely across the blanket at varied depths and slight angles, some partly in front of others, with true contact shadows, bounce light, surface reflections, realistic perspective, small fabric occlusion at the bases. Add modest flowers, sliced citrus, berries, foliage and a folded napkin woven among them. Balance wide composition with central product group; leave upper third pale and empty for dark headline copy and bottom grass dark/quiet enough for live white footer text and links. High resolution photo-realism; absolutely no extra product bottles, new branding, printed text outside products, people, UI, watermarks or border. The result should feel photographed on location in the original park rather than product cutouts placed on background.
